6. Third-party websites

Our pages link to external retailers, brands, advertising providers and other websites. Once you leave Blitzluck, the destination website's privacy policy applies. Review it before sharing personal or payment information.

7. Your choices

You can manage cookies through browser settings and, where presented, use available consent or advertising preference controls. Blocking some cookies may affect certain website features. Privacy rights and choices depend on the laws that apply where you live.

8. Children

Blitzluck is a general-audience discovery website and is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

This policy may be updated as the website, services or applicable requirements change. The date above indicates when it was last revised.
