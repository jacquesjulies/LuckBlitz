The story behind the discoveries

A world of better finds.

Blitzluck is an international product and offer discovery platform. We bring together interesting brands, useful products and selected online offers from different markets — all in one place.

Our purpose

Make discovering easier.

Shopping online can mean visiting many different websites, comparing unfamiliar brands and searching through countless product pages. Blitzluck aims to make that discovery process simpler by organising selected finds into clear categories and presenting them in one easy-to-explore destination.

Rather than focusing on just one subject, Blitzluck explores a broad range of interests. The collection can include technology and computing, travel and luggage, fashion and accessories, outdoor living, health and wellness, and other categories as new partners are added.

Our approach

Curated, clear and independent.

We aim to keep Blitzluck clean, easy to navigate and transparent about promotional links. Product descriptions and availability can change, so the retailer's website is the final source for current specifications, prices, stock, delivery destinations, returns and customer support.

Blitzluck is a discovery and referral website. Unless expressly stated otherwise, we are not the seller, manufacturer, payment processor or delivery provider for products featured on the site.

A note about affiliate links

How Blitzluck may earn.

Some links on Blitzluck are affiliate links. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase or complete another qualifying action, Blitzluck may receive a commission from the retailer or affiliate network. This is generally at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not change the price charged by the retailer.

We aim to identify promotional links clearly. Clicking an affiliate link may allow the retailer or affiliate network to record that you arrived from Blitzluck, using cookies or similar tracking technologies under their own privacy practices.

Blitzluck is an evolving collection. Categories and participating brands may change over time.
