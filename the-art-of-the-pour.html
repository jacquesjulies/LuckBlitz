The Art of Living · A Discovery Journal feature

The art of the pour.

Meet NON: a Melbourne drinks company taking a different route to the modern table, with chef-made, 0.0% alcohol-free blends built around fruit, botanicals and food.

A new point of view

Not an imitation. A category of its own.

NON approaches the wine alternative from a culinary perspective. Rather than making conventional wine and removing the alcohol afterwards, the company says it builds its drinks from the ground up using ingredients such as fruit, verjus, botanicals, spices, tannin, salinity and acidity.

The result is a collection designed to be served chilled in a wine glass and considered alongside food. It is an idea that fits a wider conversation around mindful entertaining: giving guests more thoughtful choices at the table without making the occasion feel like an afterthought.

0.0% ABV

NON describes its range as alcohol-free and never de-alcoholised. The company was founded in Melbourne in 2019 and describes its approach as chef-made.
